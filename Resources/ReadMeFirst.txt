GHOST — test build

1. Drag Ghost into Applications, then open it.

2. macOS will say it "could not verify Ghost is free of malware" — that's
   because this test build isn't notarized yet. Click Done, then:
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → "Open Anyway"
   Then open Ghost again and confirm.

3. Ghost lives in the menu bar (small cursor icon). Its setup window asks
   for two things:
     • Accessibility permission — click the button, flip Ghost on.
     • An API key — paste the one you were given into step 3, click Save.

4. Use it: in any app, tap Control twice (⌃⌃), type what you want to do,
   press Return. A cursor shows you where to click.
   Plain search is free and instant. If it can't find it, pick
   "Ask GhostBrain" and it will work out the steps.

Esc closes it. ⌃⌃ during a walkthrough cancels it.
